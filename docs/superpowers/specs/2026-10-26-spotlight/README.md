# The spotlight's look (issue #95): the kept frames

The maintainer's pick of 2026-11-01 (the design,
`../2026-10-26-asset-based-levels-design.md`, "The spotlight's look"):
a rotating warning beacon, two deep orange (`#ff6a10`) beams 180° apart,
α 10°, τ 0°, intensity 400, reach 360, a turn every 3 s, the lamp
flaring end-on (halo). Drawn by the real renderer (`olr-cli
render-match` on a scratch copy of Liero Factory Reloaded, idle bots,
seed 1, tick 275) with the beacon at the judged mount, (905, 272) under
the conveyor deck; the maintainer then moved it onto the crane.

- `view-<φ>.png`: the game's 427 × 192 view centred on (870, 300), at
  1× (rendered at 2×, halved by nearest neighbour);
- `crop-<φ>.png`: 160 × 100 world pixels around the mount at 4×;

at phases φ 0°, 45°, …, 315° of the turn (0°: the beams to the sides;
90° and 270°: end-on, the lamp flaring).
