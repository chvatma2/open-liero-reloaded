# Milestone 0.2: Scope

- **Status:** scope agreed with the user on 2026-10-16. Each sub-project
  below gets its own brainstorm, spec and plans (plans 13a, 13b, ...).
- **Baseline:** tag `v0.1.0` (`bf90d8d`), the first release under the
  name Open Liero Reloaded (OLR).

## Goal

OLR gets its own content pipeline: readable formats, and editors for
levels, mods and skins. It also gets a modern look. 0.2 ships with
**Liero Factory Reloaded** (level) and **Liero v1.0 Reloaded** (the
`olx-classic` mod), both made in our editors. The editors are also how
the graphics are tested: their previews run the real renderer and
simulation.

## Rulings that bind every sub-project

1. **Fair play.** Every player in a match sees the same picture. No
   per-player graphics or quality setting may change what is visible.
   Settings that change no information (upscale filter, window size)
   stay per-player.
2. **The look is authored content.**
   - Darkness, lights, colour grading and weapon effects are part of a
     level's or mod's identity, and its author decides them. Neither the
     player nor runtime inference does.
   - Look data ships inside the level or mod pack and is part of its
     content hash, so content download and replays carry it.
3. **Three owners.**
   - The **engine** owns the effects tied to neither a level nor a
     weapon: dirt, blood, gibs, generic particles, the HUD, chat,
     notifications and reload indicators. It may modernize them for
     everyone, vanilla content included.
   - The **level** owns the environment.
   - The **mod** owns its weapons' effects.
4. **Pixel-faithful art direction.** Lights, shadows, glow, distortion and
   particles stay on the art-scale texel grid, so the game still reads as
   pixel art.
5. **No restrictions on authors.** Players choose which content they play.
6. **The simulation stays faithful.** The oracle traces and the match
   goldens are unchanged by graphics work. The render goldens are
   re-blessed as the look changes.
7. **Old OLX content keeps working.** It imports and plays with the
   engine look.

## Sub-projects, in order

| # | Sub-project | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formats | Whether to overhaul the level, mod and skin pack formats: readability, editing by hand and by tools, room for look data. The importers follow; compatibility breaks are fine. |
| 2 | Editor foundation and level editor | Shared editor framework; create and edit levels; a live preview with the real renderer and simulation. |
| 3 | Graphics groundwork and look format | The look system and its pack data, built and tuned in the editor's preview. |
| 4 | Mod and skin editors | Weapons, projectiles and worm skins, on the editor foundation. |
| 5 | Graphics features | Specific effects, brainstormed and iterated with editor previews and intermediate playtest zips. Deliberately open until then. |
| 6 | Reloaded content and release | Liero Factory Reloaded and Liero v1.0 Reloaded; the 0.2.0 zips and a playtest. |

Each step unblocks the next. The formats come first, because the look
files, the editors and the Reloaded packs all read and write them.

## Out of scope

- Gameplay changes: new modes or weapon mechanics, and anything that
  changes the simulation.
- Bots that respect darkness. On dark levels bots see everything; this is
  a known limitation until bots get a visibility model.
- The browser version (deferred; the simulation keeps its `no_std` and
  WASM constraints).
- Per-player graphics settings.

## Done means

- Both Reloaded packs exist, were made entirely in our editors, and show
  the new look in play.
- OLX content still imports and plays.
- The oracle and the match goldens are unchanged.
- The 0.2.0 zips pass `cargo xtask dist`, and the user's playtest
  confirms the game feels the same.
